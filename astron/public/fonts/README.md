# Bundled fonts

Cinzel regular and IM Fell English regular/italic are the official Google Fonts
Latin WOFF2 files, downloaded on 2026-10-24. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are
included here. The app serves these files from its own address and precaches them
with the page, drawings and content. It makes no Google Fonts request at runtime.

Sources:
- https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Cinzel
- https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IM+Fell+English
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/cinzel
- https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/imfellenglish
