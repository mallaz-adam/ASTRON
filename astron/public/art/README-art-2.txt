ASTRON art pack 2 (static SVG). Put these files in public/art, next to the first pack.

tier-dawn, tier-morning, tier-noon, tier-corona: the four streak stages (small icons)
sun-setting: the streak is at risk (a sun low on the horizon)
sun-resting: the streak is resting (a dotted sun below the horizon, a few stars above)
new-day-plate: the night-to-morning picture for a new day

Classes you can animate with CSS: .rays (turn slowly), .halo (pulse), .twinkle (fade). All motion stops with Reduce motion.
Gold is decorative only and is never used for text.
