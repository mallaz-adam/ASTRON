# Unit 1 pictures

NASA observations, resized to a maximum of 640 pixels and compressed as WebP.
Original colours were retained. The Sun image has processed observational colours,
not a claim about how the Sun looks to unaided eyes. Do not look directly at the Sun.

- `earth.webp`: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/the-blue-marble/
  Credit: NASA Johnson Space Center. Apollo 17 photograph AS17-148-22727.
- `sun.webp`: https://science.nasa.gov/photojournal/image-of-sun-from-nasas-solar-dynamics-observatory/
  Credit: NASA/GSFC/Solar Dynamics Observatory. Image PIA26681.
- `moon.webp`: https://science.nasa.gov/resource/lunar-near-side/
  Credit: NASA/Goddard Space Flight Center/Arizona State University.
  An image made from Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter camera data.

Source and credit links are available within the lesson. NASA media terms:
https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The diagram artwork is original SVG drawn in `src/pages/UnitPictures.tsx`.
Its separate views and simplified relationships are labelled not to scale.
