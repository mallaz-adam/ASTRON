# Unit 3 observation and reconstruction assets

Retrieved on 30 October 2026 from the exact source pages below. The source
photographs and data-informed illustrations are not generated ASTRON artwork.
No recolouring is applied. Files are resized and compressed as WebP; the
edge-on illustration is also cropped vertically to remove excess blank sky.
`provenance.json` records original downloads, dimensions and the crop.

The panorama combines seven two-minute frames. Its appearance is not promised
to match the unaided eye. The solar mosaic combines multiple visible-light
observations. Galaxy images are reconstructions based on measurements, never
described as exterior photographs. The original location arrow is retained.
Interactive gold outlines are separate ASTRON teaching annotations; they do
not change the source marker or represent measured boundaries.
