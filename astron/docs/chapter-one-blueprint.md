# Chapter 1 blueprint: Our place in space

Prepared 2 November 2026. Editorial plan for review, not a new playable chapter
or a finished question bank. Uses the current stable content IDs.

## Purpose and progression

A complete beginner should finish this chapter able to place familiar objects
in the wider universe, compare scale sensibly and explain why distant views
come from the past. The six units form one progression:

**Familiar worlds → our solar system → stars and our galaxy → other galaxies
and the universe → comparing sizes and distances → interpreting travelling light.**

## Consistent unit experience

Begin with a short invitation and explain what question the unit answers.
Recall just enough earlier knowledge, then move through a connected explanation
one part at a time. Place the useful visual beside the idea it explains, followed
by a clear invitation to explore or compare. Give unfamiliar terms a definition
at first use and retain tappable definitions for review.

Guided practice should invite reasoning and explain the answer kindly. Reading
and dedicated practice remain learner-paced; no per-unit compulsory exam is
introduced. End with a recap and the next meaningful question. Use real
observations with source/credit and clearly labelled explanatory models. Keep
the main Continue action easy to reach without obscuring the final lines.

The owner's latest decision on 2 November requires an attempt and feedback on
important practice steps before Continue, without requiring a correct answer.
Keep exercises on their own steps rather than inside optional reading drawers.

## Ten-question evaluation coverage

These are authoring slots, not final learner-facing questions. Settle exact
wording, visuals, accepted answers and explanations after Units 4–6 are approved.

| Slot | Coverage | Proposed task | Why it belongs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Unit 1 | Identify roles in a familiar labelled scene. | Check the essential starting distinctions. |
| 2 | Units 1–2 | Apply those distinctions to a different arrangement of familiar bodies. | Check transfer rather than recalling a picture's position. |
| 3 | Unit 2 | Sort bodies into a solar-system relationship map. | Check organisation, not simply planet-name memory. |
| 4 | Unit 3 | Distinguish a star, a sky pattern and a galaxy from labelled examples. | Check the main categories. |
| 5 | Units 3–4 | Explain how a view from within our galaxy differs from a whole-galaxy model. | Connect viewpoint with the representation. |
| 6 | Unit 4 | Repair a nested map containing an object in the wrong level. | Apply the chapter's wider structure. |
| 7 | Unit 5 | Compare size and separation in a supplied model. | Address a likely scale confusion. |
| 8 | Units 4–5 | Choose which conclusion a not-to-scale diagram supports. | Recognise model limits. |
| 9 | Unit 6 | Order an event, light's journey and its arrival. | Check the causal sequence. |
| 10 | Units 5–6 | Use a simple supplied distance/time example to interpret a distant view. | Combine ideas in an unfamiliar but fully taught setting. |

Use a balance of straightforward and more demanding application tasks. Higher
difficulty should come from connecting ideas, not obscure vocabulary, trick
wording or untaught calculations. Include text alternatives for diagrams and
controls usable by keyboard and touch. Final choices of question widgets follow
the existing player capabilities or need a separately reviewed implementation.

## Completion and unlocking

The owner confirmed: finish the chapter's units, then complete and submit its
ten-question evaluation to unlock the next chapter. No minimum score is required.
An incomplete submission does not complete the chapter. Feedback identifies
the idea to revisit and links to the relevant unit; it does not impose an
unapproved pass mark. Optional retake details remain to be designed.

Completed chapters remain reviewable. A next chapter that lacks its authored
content must still be identified as being prepared. Development currently resets
progress on full reload; this blueprint does not change that policy or claim
the chapter gate is already implemented.
